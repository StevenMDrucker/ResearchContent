Full:

Dr. Steven M. Drucker was a Partner and Research Manager of the Visualization and Interactive Data Analysis (VIDA) group at Microsoft Research ([MSR](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/)), and is still an affiliate professor at the University of Washington Computer Science and Engineering Department ([CSE](https://www.cs.washington.edu/)). Over his 30+ year career, he published more than 120 academic papers and filed over 130 patents in areas ranging from information visualization and graphics to interfaces and interactive systems. He was inducted into the SIGCHI Academy in 2019.

Central themes in Steven's work include helping people discover meaning from data and developing methods to communicate those discoveries effectively. His analysis tools enable both analytic and intuitive exploration of large datasets by revealing underlying patterns and structures, while his communication tools help people share insights through storytelling and natural interaction techniques.

Throughout his career, Steven has been dedicated to translating academic research into practical applications. His SandDance project democratized large dataset visualization and has been incorporated into multiple Microsoft products including PowerBI, Azure Data Explorer, and Visual Studio Code. His Thumbtack project provided new tools for organizing and acting on web information. He has demonstrated his work on stage with Satya Nadella at the Microsoft CEO Summit and with Bill Gates at the Consumer Electronics Show (CES).
Steven is recognized for his diverse research contributions across fields including information visualization, storytelling, exploratory search, multi-user environments, online social interaction, hypermedia, human and robot perception, robot learning, parallel computer graphics, camera control interfaces, and spectator-oriented gaming. He has received best paper and honorable mention awards at ACM SIGCHI, ACM UIST, ACM AVI, and IEEE VIS.

He has been an active leader in the academic community, serving as Papers Chair for ACM SIGCHI 2021 & 2022, Paper Awards Chair for ACM SIGCHI 2016 & 2017, Subcommittee Chair for ACM SIGCHI 2017 & 2018, Associate Chair for numerous ACM UIST, IUI, ACM WWW, and IEEE VIS conferences, and Sponsorship Chair for VIS 2011, 2012 & 2013.

Steven received his Ph.D. from the Computer Graphics and Animation Group at the [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/) in 1994, where his thesis focused on intelligent camera control interfaces for graphical environments. He completed his M.S. at MIT's Artificial Intelligence Laboratory [CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/)researching robot learning, and graduated Magna Cum Laude with Honors in Neurosciences from [Brown University](https://www.brown.edu/) where he worked with the Brown Graphics Group. His email address is sdrucker (at) gmail.com.
